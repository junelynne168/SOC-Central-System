# SOC Central System

## Purpose

Build one connected system that finds prospects, creates a strong first impression, helps prospects create and send their first free card, converts interested prospects into customers, and supports long-term relationship marketing.

## Guiding Principle

AI is not the product. A simpler business is the product.

## Business Goal

Reduce manual prospecting, travel, repetitive demonstrations, training and follow-up while preserving personal help for qualified prospects and customers.

## Core Engines

1. Prospect Acquisition Engine
2. First Impression Engine
3. Customer Journey Engine
4. Customer Onboarding Engine
5. Relationship Generator Engine
6. AI Video Production System

## Customer Journey

1. Send a personalized First Impression Email.
2. Show the 30-second SOC introduction.
3. Invite the prospect to create their first free card.
4. Guide them with the 3-minute iPhone demonstration.
5. Help them send their first card.
6. Offer the 7-minute computer tutorial only when they prefer a computer.
7. Offer online sign-up, a phone consultation, a video meeting or an in-person appointment.

## Current Priority

Design and document the complete architecture before building individual automations.

## Build Rules

* Keep the system simple.
* Build one engine at a time.
* Do not duplicate customer data.
* Preserve human approval for sensitive communication.
* Record important decisions in Logs/Decision-Log.md.
* Do not begin coding production automations yet.
