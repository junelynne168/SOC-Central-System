# Customer Journey

## Approved Seven-Step Journey

1. Send a personalized First Impression Email.
2. Show the 30-second SOC introduction.
3. Invite the prospect to create their first free card.
4. Guide them with the 3-minute iPhone demonstration.
5. Help them send their first card.
6. Offer the 7-minute computer tutorial only when they prefer a computer.
7. Offer online sign-up, a phone consultation, a video meeting or an in-person appointment.

## Journey Rules

- The main action is **“Create Your First FREE Card.”**
- The 3-minute iPhone demonstration is the primary onboarding path.
- The 7-minute computer tutorial is optional.
- The final step is appointment or sign-up, not additional training.
- Human help remains available for qualified prospects and customers.
