# Phase 2: Business Operations

## Purpose

Define the objectives of Phase 2 for the SOC Central System.

Phase 2 focuses on operating the SOC business.

It does not redesign approved architecture.

## Phase 2 Goals

* Design daily business operations.
* Define customer follow-up rules.
* Define qualification rules.
* Define stopping rules.
* Define appointment rules.
* Define customer onboarding operations.
* Define long-term relationship operations.
* Prepare for future automation.

## Boundaries

* Preserve the approved architecture and customer journey.
* Do not create production automation during business-operations design.
* Keep operations SOC-specific, simple, explainable, and partner-ready.
* Record approved operating decisions before implementation.

## Documentation Maintenance

Every completed business-operation milestone requires:

* Dashboard update
* Decision Log update (when applicable)
* Related document updates
* Consistency verification

Documentation maintenance is part of the milestone, not an optional follow-up task.
