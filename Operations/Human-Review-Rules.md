# Human Review Rules

Human approval is required before:

- Sending sensitive or external communication when approval is required.
- Moving forward with uncertain, contradictory, or unsupported personal data.
- Resolving an exception that could affect a prospect or customer.
- Publishing or treating a communication, video, or journey asset as approved.
- Activating an integration or production automation.

Reviewers must see the destination, proposed action, source information, and relevant evidence before approval. Approval and the resulting outcome must be recorded.
