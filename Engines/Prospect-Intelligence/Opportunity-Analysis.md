# Opportunity Analysis

## Purpose

Define how verified evidence is assessed and converted into reviewable personalization opportunities.

## Analysis Questions

For each approved prospect, determine:

1. Is there a verified recent listing, sale, achievement, or professional activity?
2. Is there an approved business image connected to that opportunity?
3. Is the opportunity recent, relevant, and respectful?
4. Does the evidence clearly belong to this prospect?
5. Can the opportunity support one clear relationship-marketing angle?
6. Are there conflicts, missing facts, suppression concerns, or other review risks?

## Candidate Opportunity Record

Each candidate opportunity must include:

* Opportunity type
* Verified facts used
* Source links or references
* Source review and verification dates
* Suggested featured property, when supported
* Suggested congratulation opportunity, when supported
* Suggested personalization photo, when supported
* Suggested opening theme
* Suggested relationship angle
* Confidence level
* Confidence explanation
* Known limitations or conflicts
* Human-review status

## Opportunity Priority

Prefer opportunities that are:

* Supported by reliable, current evidence
* Clearly connected to the approved prospect
* Professionally relevant
* Easy for a reviewer to understand
* Suitable for one clear First Impression direction

Do not force an opportunity when the evidence is insufficient. An approved prospect may produce no usable personalization recommendation.

## Output Rule

The analysis produces recommendations only. It does not draft customer-facing content, authorize contact, select a final strategy, or send communication.
