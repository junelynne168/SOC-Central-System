# Intelligence Workflow

## Workflow

1. Approved prospect record received
2. Approval, duplicate, and suppression status confirmed
3. Verified facts and supporting evidence separated from unusable information
4. Candidate personalization opportunities identified
5. Opportunities analyzed and scored
6. Recommended strategy, confidence level, and evidence prepared
7. Human review performed
8. Recommendation approved, rejected, deferred, or marked incomplete
9. Approved intelligence package moves to the First Impression Engine

## Review Outcomes

### Approved

The reviewer selects a recommendation for use by the First Impression Engine. This does not authorize contact or sending.

### Rejected

The recommendation is unsuitable, unsupported, unsafe, or not meaningful. Record the reason.

### Deferred

The recommendation may be useful later, but review or use is postponed. Record the reason and any approved condition.

### Incomplete

Required evidence or analysis is missing, contradictory, or unverified. Record what is needed and do not invent it.

## Required Reviewer View

The reviewer must see:

* Prospect identifier and name
* Prospect approval status
* Suppression status
* Suggested featured property
* Suggested congratulation opportunity
* Suggested personalization photo
* Suggested opening theme
* Suggested relationship angle
* Confidence level and explanation
* Supporting facts, each labeled as verified
* AI suggestions, each labeled as a suggestion
* Source links or references
* Score and category explanations
* Known limitations, conflicts, or risks

## Human Review Gate

Only a human-approved intelligence package may move to the First Impression Engine. This engine never generates or sends outbound communication.
