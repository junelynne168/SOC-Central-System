# Personalization Scoring

## Purpose

Provide a proposed scoring method for comparing personalization opportunities and communicating confidence to a human reviewer.

## Proposed Opportunity Score

| Category | Maximum points | Review question |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence reliability | 30 | Are the supporting facts verified by reliable, recorded sources? |
| Prospect identity match | 20 | Does the evidence clearly belong to the approved prospect? |
| Recency | 15 | Is the opportunity current enough to remain meaningful? |
| Professional relevance | 15 | Does it relate directly to the prospect’s visible business activity? |
| Relationship value | 10 | Can it support a respectful relationship-marketing angle? |
| Visual suitability | 10 | Is there an approved, relevant business image that may support personalization? |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Proposed Opportunity Bands

* **80–100:** Strong opportunity for human review
* **60–79:** Usable opportunity with reviewer judgment
* **40–59:** Weak or incomplete opportunity
* **Below 40:** Do not recommend without additional approved evidence

## Confidence Levels

### High

Material facts are verified, current, internally consistent, clearly tied to the prospect, and directly support the recommendation.

### Medium

Core facts are verified, but recency, completeness, visual suitability, or relationship relevance has a documented limitation.

### Low

Evidence is limited or uncertain. The recommendation must not advance without additional approved evidence and human review.

## Scoring Rules

* Score only approved information.
* Record the evidence and explanation for every category.
* Missing information receives no inferred credit.
* Distinguish the calculated score from the human decision.
* A high score does not authorize contact or override suppression and safety rules.
* The score supports human review; it does not select final customer-facing content.

## Status

This scoring design is provisional and requires June’s approval before implementation.
