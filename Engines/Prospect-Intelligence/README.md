# Prospect Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

Analyze approved prospect records and recommend the most meaningful personalization strategy before the First Impression Engine prepares customer-facing content.

## Business Goal

Turn verified prospect information into clear, relevant First Impression opportunities while preserving human judgment and preventing invented personalization.

## Inputs

* Approved prospect record
* Verified source evidence
* Prospect score and explanation
* Duplicate and suppression results
* Approved personalization and review rules

## Outputs

* Suggested featured property
* Suggested congratulation opportunity
* Suggested personalization photo
* Suggested opening theme
* Suggested relationship angle
* Confidence level
* Supporting evidence

Each output must identify whether it is a verified fact or an AI suggestion.

## Boundaries

* Use only approved prospect information.
* Never invent personal facts.
* Distinguish verified facts from AI suggestions.
* Recommendations support human review.
* No outbound communication is generated by this engine.
* Do not scrape, connect to live sources, or build production automation.
* Do not change the approved prospect record or suppression status.

## Design Documents

* `Personalization-Strategy.md` — Approved information use and recommendation types.
* `Opportunity-Analysis.md` — Opportunity selection, evidence, and limitations.
* `Personalization-Scoring.md` — Proposed recommendation and confidence scoring.
* `Intelligence-Workflow.md` — Analysis and human-review stages.
* `Intelligence-Data-Flow.md` — Primary flow, handoff, and exception paths.
