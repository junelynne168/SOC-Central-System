# AI Video Production System

## Purpose

Produce and maintain approved video guidance for the SOC customer journey.

## Inputs

- Approved scripts
- Demonstration requirements
- Brand and review requirements

## Outputs

- 30-second SOC introduction
- 3-minute iPhone demonstration
- Optional 7-minute computer tutorial
- Future approved journey videos

## Boundary

No video is treated as approved or published without review evidence and June’s approval.
