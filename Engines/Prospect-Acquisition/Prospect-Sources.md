# Prospect Sources

## Purpose

Define placeholders for potential permitted public sources. This document does not establish legal permission, scraping permission, API access, credentials, or production access.

## Source Placeholders

| Public source | Information expected | Reliability concerns | Update frequency | Permitted use | Verification requirements |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Realtor and brokerage websites | Name, brokerage, role, market, business contact details, listings, biography | Pages may be outdated, incomplete, or controlled by different parties | Check when reviewing a candidate | Manual review of publicly displayed business information, subject to approved policy | Confirm identity, brokerage, business purpose of contact details, page URL or reference, and review date |
| Public property listing pages | Agent name, brokerage, listing activity, market, property and listing dates | Listings may be stale, syndicated, duplicated, withdrawn, or attributed inconsistently | Check when activity evidence is collected | Manual evidence for visible recent business activity, subject to approved policy | Confirm listing status, date, agent identity, brokerage, and a second source when facts conflict |
| Zillow or similar public Realtor profiles | Agent profile, service area, activity, reviews, business contact information | Platform data may be self-reported, delayed, incomplete, or aggregated | Check when reviewing a candidate | Manual review of public business-profile information, subject to platform terms and approved policy | Confirm identity and material facts against an independent source |
| Realtor.com public profiles | Agent profile, brokerage, market, activity, reviews, business contact information | Data may be self-reported, syndicated, incomplete, or outdated | Check when reviewing a candidate | Manual review of public business-profile information, subject to platform terms and approved policy | Confirm identity, activity dates, and business contact details against another source |
| Brokerage agent directories | Name, brokerage affiliation, office, market, biography, business contact details | Departed agents or old office details may remain listed | Check when reviewing a candidate | Manual confirmation of public professional affiliation and contact details | Confirm the directory is official, current, and consistent with another source |
| Public Facebook business pages | Business identity, posts, branding, activity, market, public business contact details | Personal and business pages may be confused; posts may not establish transactions | Check only when needed for current evidence | Manual review of clearly public business content, subject to approved policy and platform terms | Confirm it is a business presence, record the review date, and do not use personal-only information |
| Public Instagram business profiles | Business identity, public posts, branding, activity, market, public business contact details | Accounts may be personal, inactive, impersonated, or lack verifiable business facts | Check only when needed for current evidence | Manual review of clearly public business content, subject to approved policy and platform terms | Confirm identity through an official website or brokerage source; exclude personal-only contact details |
| Public LinkedIn profiles | Professional identity, brokerage, role, location, visible activity | Profiles are self-reported and may be outdated or incomplete | Check when professional identity requires confirmation | Manual review of public professional information, subject to approved policy and platform terms | Confirm current affiliation and business facts through another public source |
| Public Google business listings | Business name, location, website, phone, hours, reviews | User edits, duplicate listings, wrong locations, and review manipulation are possible | Check when location or business presence is reviewed | Manual review of public business listing information, subject to approved policy | Confirm the listing belongs to the prospect and corroborate contact details and location |
| Local real estate association directories | Membership identity, professional affiliation, market, public directory contact details | Directory access and permitted use vary; records may lag membership changes | Check when an approved directory is used | Manual review only when public access and intended use are confirmed | Confirm the directory is official, information is current, and use is permitted |
| Publicly available recent-sale information | Visible sale activity, dates, market, represented property or transaction context | Sale data may be delayed, incomplete, syndicated, or unclear about representation | Check when recent activity is evaluated | Manual evidence of visible activity, subject to approved policy | Confirm date, property context, agent identity, and consistency with a reliable second source |

## Source Rules

* Use only sources approved for the intended purpose.
* Record a source link or stable reference and the date reviewed.
* Distinguish verified facts from unverified claims.
* Do not infer that public visibility automatically permits collection, automation, or contact.
* Do not invent APIs, credentials, scraping permissions, legal conclusions, or missing information.
* Stop and request human review when sources conflict or permitted use is uncertain.
