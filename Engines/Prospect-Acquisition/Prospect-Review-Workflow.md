# Prospect Review Workflow

## Workflow

1. Candidate discovered
2. Source evidence collected
3. Duplicate check completed
4. Required fields validated
5. Prospect score calculated
6. Human review performed
7. Prospect approved, rejected, deferred, or marked incomplete
8. Approved prospect moves to First Impression Engine

No First Impression Email may be prepared for sending until the prospect is approved through this workflow.

## Review Outcomes

### Approved

Required evidence is sufficient, duplicate and suppression checks are acceptable, and the reviewer authorizes movement to the First Impression Engine. This outcome does not itself authorize external contact.

### Rejected

The candidate does not meet approved criteria, is excluded, or must not advance. Record the reason.

### Deferred

The candidate may be suitable, but review is intentionally postponed. Record the reason and any approved review date or condition.

### Incomplete

Required information or evidence is missing, contradictory, unavailable, or unverified. Record what is needed; do not invent it.

## Required Reviewer View

The reviewer must see:

* Prospect name
* Brokerage
* Business location
* Public business contact information
* Source links or references
* Recent activity evidence
* Score and explanation
* Duplicate result
* Suppression status
* Proposed personalization opportunity

## Review Record

Record the reviewer, review timestamp, outcome, reason, unresolved questions, and approved next action in the authoritative prospect record.
