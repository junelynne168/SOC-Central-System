# Prospect Criteria

## Purpose

Define the provisional criteria used to identify residential Realtor prospects for human review.

## Primary Target

Residential real estate agents who:

* Have recently sold or listed homes
* Are active in their local market
* Have a visible public business presence
* Use professional photos, listing photos, or social media
* Have accessible business contact information
* Could benefit from relationship marketing and referral generation

## Useful Qualification Signals

* Recent home sales
* Recent listings
* Consistent business activity
* Public website
* Public business email
* Active Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Realtor profile
* Positive client reviews
* Visible personal branding
* Geographic area served
* Brokerage affiliation

## Exclusion Signals

* No recent visible activity
* Missing or unreliable contact information
* Duplicate prospect
* Prospect already contacted
* Existing SOC customer
* Suppression or do-not-contact status
* Information that cannot be verified
* Personal contact information not clearly intended for business use

## Approval Status

These criteria are provisional and require June’s approval before production use. They support human review and do not authorize contact.
