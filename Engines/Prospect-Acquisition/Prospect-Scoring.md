# Prospect Scoring

## Purpose

Provide a proposed 100-point framework that helps reviewers prioritize complete, evidence-supported prospect records.

## Proposed Categories

| Category | Maximum points | Proposed scoring considerations |
|---|---:|---|
| Recent business activity | 25 | Recency, visibility, and consistency of verified listings or sales |
| Public contact quality | 20 | Clearly business-intended, current, and corroborated contact information |
| Visible marketing activity | 15 | Current professional website, business social presence, listing presentation, or branding |
| Personalization opportunity | 15 | Verified, respectful business context that can support relevant personalization |
| Local market relevance | 10 | Verified geographic fit and active service in the intended market |
| Reputation and reviews | 10 | Visible, relevant, and reasonably current public review evidence |
| Data confidence | 5 | Completeness, source quality, consistency, and recency of the record |
| **Total** | **100** | |

Detailed point rules within each category remain provisional and require approval before implementation.

## Proposed Score Bands

* **80–100:** High-priority review
* **60–79:** Standard review
* **40–59:** Low-priority or incomplete
* **Below 40:** Do not advance without additional evidence

## Scoring Rules

* The score supports human review.
* The score does not authorize contact.
* Missing information must not be invented.
* A high score does not override suppression or safety rules.
* Record the evidence and explanation for every awarded category.
* Contradictory or unverified facts must not receive full credit.
* Human review determines the final outcome.

## Status

This scoring framework is proposed design only and requires June’s approval before production use.
