# First 60 Seconds Experience

## Purpose

Design the visitor's emotional journey during the first minute after arriving.

This is NOT a webpage.

It is a story.

The objective:

Make the visitor excited enough that they WANT to create their first FREE card.

## Scene 1 (0–10 sec)

Emotion:

Curiosity

Screen:

Headline

Create a card your client will smile at,  
keep,  
and remember—for you.

Large Hero Button

Create My First FREE Card

Customer Question:

Why should I care?

## Scene 2 (10–20 sec)

Emotion:

Surprise

Visual Story

Ordinary Realtor Marketing

↓

Trash

vs.

Personalized Card

↓

Refrigerator

Minimal text.

Maximum visual impact.

Customer Question:

Why is this different?

## Scene 3 (20–35 sec)

Confidence

Simple flow

↓

↓

③ Send it

Can I really do this?

## Scene 4 (35–50 sec)

Emotion:

Relief

Three confidence badges

✔ About 5 Minutes

✔ Works on iPhone

✔ First Card FREE

Customer Question:

Is this easy?

## Scene 5 (50–60 sec)

Emotion:

Excitement

Headline

Ready to create something unforgettable?

Large Button

Create My First FREE Card

Customer Question:

Let's do it.

## Storyboard Rules

Every scene should answer ONE emotional question.

Every scroll should create ONE emotional reaction.

No unnecessary paragraphs.

No technical explanations.

No feature list.

Design emotion before design graphics.
