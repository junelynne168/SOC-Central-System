## Landing Page Design Rules

Keep the page:

- Simple
- Emotional
- Friendly
- Mobile-first
- Free of technical explanations
- Focused on creating excitement

The page must answer these questions:

1. Why should I care?
2. Why is this different?
3. Can I really do this?
4. Let's try it.
